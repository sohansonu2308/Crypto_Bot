# Institutional Crypto Bull-Run Playbook (v2 — Whale Edition)

This reference playbook grounds daily signal evaluation against objective institutional flow, market structure, and derivatives positioning across the 2017, 2020–21, and 2023–24 cycles.

---

## 1. Pure Fiat Dry-Powder Velocity & Macro Liquidity

Genuine bull runs begin at the **second derivative of fiat liquidity**—the moment fiat stops leaving the ecosystem and begins expanding—not when social media turns bullish.

- **Pure Fiat Stables (`USDT + USDC`) vs. Yield Stables (`USDe`):**
  - Synthetic/basis stables (`USDe`) mechanically shrink when perp funding turns negative. Always judge real dry powder using **`USDT + USDC` combined supply**.
  - **2nd-Derivative Inflection:** Bull runs start when `USDT + USDC` 7d supply change accelerates from negative (`-1.0%` → `-0.2%`) into sustained positive net minting (`>+0.3%` to `>+1.5%` weekly).
- **Real-Time `USDT/USD` Peg Thermometer:**
  - `USDT/USD < $0.9993` (`< -7 bps`): Active fiat redemptions — holders are selling USDT for USD wire withdrawals (liquidity drain).
  - `USDT/USD > $1.0002` (`> +2 bps`): Aggressive fiat inflows — institutions/desks buying USDT faster than redemptions.
- **US Macro Net Liquidity (`Fed Balance Sheet - TGA - RRP`):**
  - Positive 4-week expansion in US Net Dollar Liquidity acts as a macro tailwind; sharp contractions cap crypto breakouts into range-bound traps.

## 2. Multi-Timeframe Spot Structure & The Coinbase Spot Premium

1. **Moving Average Regime (`20D / 50D / 200D SMA`):**
   - **Markdown:** BTC below falling `50D` and `200D SMA`. Every low-volume bounce into a falling `20D/50D SMA` is suspect until proven otherwise.
   - **Ignition Trigger:** BTC reclaims `20D` and `50D SMA` (or breaks a 20–30 day Donchian range high) on **`>= 1.5x–2.0x` 20-day average USD spot volume**.
   - **Confirmed Bull Trend:** BTC holds above a rising `50D` and `200D SMA`, with pullbacks finding support above higher lows.
2. **Coinbase Spot Premium (`Coinbase BTC-USD` vs. Offshore `BTC-USDT`):**
   - Every genuine US institutional / ETF accumulation wave (2020, Oct 2023, Jan/Oct 2024) printed a persistent **positive Coinbase USD premium (`+3 bps` to `+25 bps`)**.
   - When BTC spikes `+6%` while Coinbase trades at a **discount** to offshore USDT perps, it is almost always an offshore short squeeze (`Bull Trap`).

## 4. Capital Rotation & Benchmark Breadth vs. Shill Froth

Sustainable bull cycles follow strict capital hierarchy:
1. **BTC Leadership (`BTC.D` firm/rising, `> 55%`):** Capital enters BTC first; `ETH/BTC` lags or stabilizes.
2. **Liquid Majors Breadth:** Top liquid institutional assets (`SOL, LINK, AAVE, SUI, AVAX, TAO, ONDO`) begin turning positive over 7d alongside BTC.
3. **Late-Stage Froth Warning:** If Telegram KOL channels aggressively pump illiquid micro-caps (`FARTCOIN`, `TIBBIR`, `PUMP`) while BTC is below its `50D SMA`, `USDT+USDC` is shrinking, and Liquid Majors breadth is `< 30%`, those micro-cap pumps are **pure exit-liquidity extraction**, not altseason.

## 5. Telegram KOL Channels as a Contrarian & Apathy Gauge

Public Telegram channels **never** lead institutional accumulation. Treat them behaviorally:
- **Retail Apathy (Bullish Bottom Precursor):** When `median views/post` and `posts/day` drop `< 0.65x` of their 60-day baseline while Fear & Greed is `< 20` and `USDT+USDC` outflows decelerate, retail sellers are exhausted.
- **Promo / Hindsight Spam Discount:** High density of `VIP`, `premium`, `win rate`, or cherry-picked victory laps has **zero predictive value** and must be discounted completely.

## 6. Six-Phase Institutional Cycle Rubric

Never confuse late-bear **Markdown** (at `Fear & Greed < 25`) with cycle-top **Distribution** (at `Fear & Greed > 70`):

1. **`Markdown / Liquidity Drain`:** Price below `50D/200D SMA`, `USDT+USDC` contracting, negative `USDT/USD` peg (`< -5 bps`), weak breadth. Capital is actively exiting crypto.
2. **`Capitulation / Bottoming Watch`:** Extreme Fear (`< 20`), washed-out/negative perp funding & basis, rising Telegram view apathy, and `USDT+USDC` outflows beginning to decelerate—watch closely for spot absorption, but breakout is not yet confirmed.
3. **`Stealth Accumulation`:** Multi-week range base holding support, `USDT+USDC` turning flat-to-positive, Coinbase Spot Premium turning positive, funding quiet, retail still fearful/apathetic.
4. **`Early Bull Ignition`:** Objective breakout above `20D/50D` structure on expanding spot volume (`>= 1.5x` 20D avg), positive `USDT+USDC` velocity, positive Coinbase Spot Premium, and uncrowded perp funding ("disbelief rally").
5. **`Confirmed Bull Trend`:** Price sustained above rising `50D/200D SMA`, steady fiat stablecoin inflows, expanding DeFi TVL, and broadening participation across liquid majors.
6. **`Bull Trap / Distribution`:** Either (a) a perp-driven counter-trend bounce on weak spot volume, negative Coinbase premium, and shrinking fiat stables, OR (b) late-cycle euphoria (`Fear & Greed > 75`, overheated funding `> 0.04%`, rampant micro-cap shilling while BTC momentum stalls).
