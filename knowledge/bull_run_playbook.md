# Historical Crypto Bull Run Playbook (2017, 2020–21, 2023–24)

This reference playbook grounds daily signal evaluation against objective structural patterns observed across prior crypto bull cycles. Use it to separate genuine regime shifts from leverage-driven bull traps and Telegram hype.

---

## 1. Macro Liquidity Shifts & Stablecoin Supply Inflection Points

Genuine crypto bull runs are preceded or confirmed by expanding net liquidity, not isolated social media narratives.

- **2017 Cycle:** Broad global risk-on liquidity, weak USD, and rapid fiat on-ramp expansion (Coinbase, Binance, Korean exchanges) drove sustained spot accumulation across BTC first, then ETH/ICOs.
- **2020–21 Cycle:** Massive post-COVID central bank balance sheet expansion and fiscal stimulus coincided with **USDT + USDC market cap surging from ~$5B to >$100B**. Sustained multi-week positive 7d stablecoin net issuance preceded every major leg higher.
- **2023–24 Cycle:** Global M2 stabilization, US spot Bitcoin ETF approvals (Jan 2024), and **stablecoin supply bottoming in Q4 2023 before expanding steadily through 2024** marked the real structural shift from bear-market relief rallies to a confirmed bull trend.

### Key Confirmation Signals
- **Bullish Confirmation:** 7d stablecoin market cap net change consistently positive (`>+0.5%` to `>+2%` weekly) alongside rising DeFi TVL and expanding spot volume.
- **Trap / Bear-Market Rally Warning:** Price rallies while total stablecoin market cap is flat or shrinking (`<= 0%` 7d) and DeFi TVL stagnates — indicates capital is merely rotating internally or relying on perp leverage.

## 2. Capital Rotation Sequence

Across all three cycles, sustainable bull markets follow a recognizable capital cascade rather than random simultaneous altcoin pumps:

1. **Phase 1 — BTC Breakout & Dominance Expansion (`BTC.D` Rising):**
   - Institutional and macro capital enters Bitcoin first.
   - BTC breaks multi-month resistance on heavy spot volume while most altcoins lag in BTC pairs (`ALT/BTC` bleeds).
2. **Phase 2 — `BTC.D` Peak & ETH Catch-Up:**
   - BTC consolidates near local highs; `BTC.D` stalls or begins rolling over.
   - ETH outperforms BTC (`ETH/BTC` reverses upward) on expanding spot volume and improving ecosystem TVL.
3. **Phase 3 — High-Cap Altcoin Rotation:**
   - Capital rotates from BTC/ETH into liquid top-20 L1/L2 and infrastructure leaders (e.g., SOL, AVAX, LINK, ARB/OP, SUI).
4. **Phase 4 — Sector & Narrative Rotation:**
   - Sustained multi-week bids rotate across specific themes: **AI**, **RWA (Real World Assets)**, **DeFi**, **L1/L2**, and eventually **Memes**.
   - Healthy rotation shows rising 7d spot volume across multiple assets in a sector, not a single illiquid ticker pumping 40% in 2 hours.
5. **Phase 5 — Blow-Off / Distribution:**
   - Retail euphoria peaks (Fear & Greed `> 80` for weeks), micro-cap memes dominate Telegram feeds, `BTC.D` drops sharply while BTC fails to make higher highs, and perp funding becomes chronically overheated.

## 3. Spot Volume Expansion vs. Leverage-Driven Perp Pumps

Distinguishing real spot demand from derivatives-driven squeezes is the single most reliable bull-trap filter:

| Metric | Genuine Bull Ignition / Trend | Leverage-Driven Bull Trap / Fakeout |
|---|---|---|
| **Spot Volume** | Sustained multi-day expansion across BTC, ETH, and major pairs | Flat or declining spot volume during price spikes |
| **Perp Funding Rates** | Neutral to mildly positive (`0.005%` to `0.02%` per 8h) | Sudden spike (`> 0.04%–0.05%+` per 8h) as late longs pile in |
| **Open Interest (OI)** | Rises gradually alongside spot accumulation | Vertical OI surge outpacing spot volume — fragile cascade risk |
| **Price Structure** | Pullbacks bought quickly on spot; higher lows hold | Sharp vertical wick up followed by swift mean reversion / long liquidation |

## 4. Classic Telegram & Retail Manipulation Signatures

Public Telegram alpha/news channels frequently amplify noise or serve as exit liquidity. Watch for these patterns:

1. **Coordinated Cross-Channel Shilling:**
   - Multiple unrelated channels posting near-identical bullish copy, identical chart screenshots, or the same low/mid-cap ticker within a 1–6 hour window.
   - Often accompanies paid promotions or syndicate exit liquidity.
2. **Unverified Insider / ETF / Partnership Rumors:**
   - Claims of "imminent Tier-1 CEX listing", "secret BlackRock/government deal", or "confirmed ETF approval" without primary-source links (SEC filings, official project announcements, on-chain proof).
3. **Exit-Liquidity Hype on Thin-Volume Altcoins:**
   - Channels hyping an altcoin after it has already pumped `+25%` to `+80%` on thin volume, or pushing a token whose 24h spot volume is tiny relative to claimed "whale accumulation".
4. **Sentiment Lag & Engagement Farming:**
   - Extreme FOMO ("Supercycle confirmed!", "Last chance before $200k!") appearing *after* a vertical move into resistance, or extreme FUD ("Crypto is dead") *after* a major liquidation flush at support.
   - Forwarded screenshots of fake PnL cards or cherry-picked past calls with zero mention of invalidated calls.

## 5. Verdict Classification Rubric

- **`Accumulation`:** Sideways/base-building price action, low-to-neutral funding, muted retail sentiment (Fear & Greed `< 45`), early stablecoin stabilization, no confirmed breakout yet.
- **`Early Ignition`:** BTC breaking key range highs on expanding spot volume, stablecoin 7d supply turning positive, funding still healthy/moderate, `BTC.D` firm or rising, retail still skeptical.
- **`Confirmed Bull Trend`:** Sustained multi-week uptrend in BTC & total market cap, positive stablecoin inflows, healthy DeFi TVL growth, orderly capital rotation into ETH and major sectors without terminal blow-off exhaustion.
- **`Bull Trap / Fakeout`:** Sharp upward price move driven predominantly by perp leverage (spiking funding/OI) on weak spot volume, flat/negative stablecoin flows, and heavy Telegram FOMO/shilling without macro confirmation.
- **`Distribution / Noise`:** Choppy, news-driven range or late-stage euphoria where Telegram hype and micro-cap shilling surge while BTC stalls, spot volume fades, or smart money distributes into retail bids.
